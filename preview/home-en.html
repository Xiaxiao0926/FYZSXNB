How trust is built

Source first. Limits visible. Solutions second.

Chinese communities, product pages and manufacturer materials often contain the original signal. We preserve that value, then verify high-risk claims with evidence appropriate to the decision.

  1. Discover. Find the exact model, claim, user problem or market gap in its original context.
  2. Verify. Check compatibility, medical, regulatory, safety and policy claims against suitable sources.
  3. Explain. Separate confirmed facts, manufacturer claims, community experience, inference and unknowns.
  4. Connect. Only then map the evidence to a Chinese product, supplier route or practical next step.
